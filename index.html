About us

Serving Ikorodu and its surrounding communities

MTN Alabukun is an authorized MTN Data Partner centre based at Ikorodu Garage, Lagos. We help customers across Ikorodu and the surrounding areas with SIM registration, SIM swap, eSIM activation, MTN broadband devices, and Samsung Galaxy A Series smartphones.

Our goal is simple: make MTN products and services easy to access, easy to understand, and easy to order — whether you visit us in person or reach us on WhatsApp.
